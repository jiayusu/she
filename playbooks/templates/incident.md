# Incident: <stable fingerprint>

## Fingerprint

Record the exact error, failed assertion, or observable symptom.

## Environment

Record OS, runtime, component, command, and relevant versions without secrets.

## Symptoms

Describe what failed and what still worked.

## Root cause

State the evidence-backed cause, not a suspected fix.

## Attempts that did not work

List attempts and the evidence that rejected them. Use `none` when applicable.

## Resolution

Give the smallest corrective change or operational procedure.

## Verification

Record the exact reproduction and passing regression commands.

## Prevention

State the automated guard, API change, or review check that prevents recurrence.

## Skill decision

Choose `existing skill`, `promote after repetition`, or `keep as incident`, with a reason.
