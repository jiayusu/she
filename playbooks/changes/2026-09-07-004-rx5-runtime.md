camera-lifecycle safety, fixed local fallback, strict log redaction, guarded RDK

## Reusable knowledge

Camera access belongs inside an explicit command scope and must close in a
`finally` block. Capability discovery reports structured absence rather than
crashing or synthesizing success. This remains a playbook—not a skill—until the
procedure succeeds on real hardware more than once.
